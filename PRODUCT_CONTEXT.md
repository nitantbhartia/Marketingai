# ClaimCoach — Product Context

## What ClaimCoach DOES:
- Analyzes auto insurance total loss settlement offers
- Identifies commonly missed or undervalued line items
- Shows users specific dollar amounts they may be owed
- Provides state-specific information about insurance regulations
- Generates analysis reports users can reference when talking to adjusters

## What ClaimCoach does NOT do:
- Does NOT negotiate with insurance companies on your behalf
- Does NOT provide legal advice
- Does NOT guarantee any specific dollar recovery
- Does NOT file claims or disputes for you
- Does NOT replace a public adjuster or attorney
- Does NOT analyze health insurance, homeowner's, or non-auto claims
- Does NOT access insurance company systems or databases

## Common Line Items ClaimCoach Identifies:
- Sales tax on replacement vehicle ($800–$3,000+)
- Title, registration, and transfer fees ($200–$500)
- Comparable vehicle adjustments (mileage, condition) ($500–$2,000)
- Dealer fees and documentation charges ($300–$800)
- Loss of use / rental car gap ($200–$1,500)
- Aftermarket modifications and upgrades (varies)
- State-specific items (varies by state)

## Approved Claims (can use in content):
- "Most total loss offers are missing line items you're entitled to"
- "ClaimCoach helps you understand what your offer should include"
- "See what adjusters commonly leave off settlement offers"

## NEVER Claim:
- Specific average recovery amounts (unless backed by real user data)
- "Get thousands more" or similar promissory language
- That ClaimCoach's analysis is legally binding
- That using ClaimCoach guarantees a higher settlement
