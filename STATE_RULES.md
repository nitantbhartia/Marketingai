# State-Specific Insurance Rules Reference

## California (CA)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** Owner-retained threshold at 100% of ACV (actual cash value); insurer may declare total loss earlier
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims (not first-party)
- **Dispute Timeline:** 30 days to file complaint with DOI after final denial
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Sales tax reimbursement required on replacement vehicle
- **Loss of Use:** Entitled to loss of use if not at fault
- **DOI Website:** https://www.insurance.ca.gov
- **Key Statutes:** CA Insurance Code §2695.8(b)

## Texas (TX)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** 100% of ACV
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims
- **Dispute Timeline:** No specific statutory deadline; file complaint with TDI
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Sales tax and title fees must be included in settlement
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://www.tdi.texas.gov
- **Key Statutes:** TX Insurance Code Chapter 542

## Florida (FL)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** 80% of ACV (statutory threshold)
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims; difficult for first-party
- **Dispute Timeline:** 60 days for insurer to respond to claim
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Required in total loss settlements
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://www.floir.com
- **Key Statutes:** FL Statute §627.7276

## New York (NY)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** 75% of ACV
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims
- **Dispute Timeline:** Insurer must acknowledge claim within 15 business days
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Sales tax reimbursement required
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://www.dfs.ny.gov
- **Key Statutes:** NY Insurance Regulation 64 (11 NYCRR 216)

## Pennsylvania (PA)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** No statutory percentage; insurer discretion
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims
- **Dispute Timeline:** Insurer must respond within 10 business days
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Required in settlements
- **Loss of Use:** Available if not at fault
- **DOI Website:** https://www.insurance.pa.gov
- **Key Statutes:** 31 PA Code §146.5

## Illinois (IL)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** No statutory percentage; insurer discretion
- **Diminished Value:** Limited availability; case-by-case basis
- **Dispute Timeline:** Insurer must respond within 30 days
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Sales tax reimbursement required
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://insurance.illinois.gov
- **Key Statutes:** 215 ILCS 5/154.6

## Ohio (OH)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** No statutory percentage; insurer discretion
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims
- **Dispute Timeline:** Insurer must acknowledge within 15 days
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Required in total loss settlements
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://insurance.ohio.gov
- **Key Statutes:** OH Admin Code 3901-1-54

## Georgia (GA)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** 100% of ACV (but insurer may settle earlier)
- **Diminished Value:** Strong diminished value state — available even for first-party claims (State Farm Mutual v. Mabry)
- **Dispute Timeline:** No specific statutory deadline
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Required (title ad valorem tax)
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://oci.georgia.gov
- **Key Statutes:** O.C.G.A. §33-4-6

## North Carolina (NC)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** 75% of ACV
- **Diminished Value:** Available for third-party claims
- **Dispute Timeline:** Insurer must respond within 30 days
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Required (Highway Use Tax)
- **Loss of Use:** Available in third-party claims
- **DOI Website:** https://www.ncdoi.gov
- **Key Statutes:** NC Gen. Stat. §58-36-75

## Michigan (MI)
- **Total Loss Threshold:** 75% of ACV
- **Diminished Value:** Limited due to no-fault system; available in mini-tort claims
- **Dispute Timeline:** Insurer must respond within 60 days
- **Sales Tax Recovery:** Required in settlements
- **Loss of Use:** Complex due to no-fault; PIP covers some expenses
- **DOI Website:** https://www.michigan.gov/difs
- **Key Statutes:** MCL 500.3145

---

## Notes for Content Writers
- Always cite the specific statute when referencing state rules
- Include the DOI website as an external link
- State rules change — verify current applicability before publishing
- When in doubt, recommend the reader verify with their state DOI or consult an attorney
- Never present state rules as universal — always specify which state
