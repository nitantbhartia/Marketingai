# ClaimCoach Product Context

This file defines what ClaimCoach DOES and DOES NOT do.
Used by the Product Claim Validator to prevent feature hallucinations.

## What ClaimCoach DOES

✅ **Analysis Only**
- Analyzes total loss settlement offers
- Identifies missing line items (sales tax, title, registration, loss of use, etc.)
- Shows what insurers commonly omit or undervalue
- Helps users understand if their settlement is fair
- Takes under 5 minutes to analyze an offer

✅ **Education**
- Explains insurance concepts in plain English
- Shows typical ranges for common line items
- Provides state-specific guidance
- Helps users understand their rights

✅ **Free Analysis**
- No credit card required for initial analysis
- No commitment needed

## What ClaimCoach DOES NOT DO

❌ **Does NOT Negotiate**
- Does not negotiate with insurers on your behalf
- Does not contact adjusters
- Does not communicate with insurance companies for you

❌ **Does NOT File Claims/Disputes**
- Does not file claims for you
- Does not submit dispute letters for you
- Does not generate demand letters or counter-offer letters

❌ **Does NOT Have Document Upload**
- No file upload feature
- Cannot analyze PDFs or images
- No document parsing

❌ **Does NOT Track Claims**
- No claim status tracking
- No notifications or alerts
- No follow-up monitoring

❌ **Does NOT Provide Legal Advice**
- Is not a law firm
- Does not provide legal representation
- Cannot give legal advice

❌ **Does NOT Integrate with Insurer Systems**
- No CCC ONE access
- No Mitchell integration
- No direct insurance system connections

❌ **Does NOT Have Chat/Assistant**
- No chatbot feature
- No AI assistant to answer questions
- Analysis is one-time, not interactive

## Line Item Typical Ranges

These are the ranges ClaimCoach shows users for common settlement line items:

| Line Item | Typical Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Sales Tax | 6% - 10% of ACV (varies by state) |
| Title Fee | $15 - $200 (varies by state) |
| Registration | $50 - $500 (varies by state and vehicle type) |
| Loss of Use | $30 - $60/day for 7-14 days |
| Dealer Fee | $150 - $500 (if applicable) |
| Towing/Storage | $100 - $400 (if incurred) |
| Aftermarket Equipment | Actual value with documentation |

- Do NOT guarantee success rates
