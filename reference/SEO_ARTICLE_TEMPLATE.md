# SEO Article Template (ClaimCoach)

Use this template for every Quill draft and revision.

## Goal
- Rank for the target keyword while converting readers who suspect a lowball total-loss offer.
- Keep content practical, specific, and state-accurate.

## Required Structure
1. `# H1` with exact target keyword.
2. Intro (80-140 words):
   - Include keyword in first 100 words.
   - Give a direct answer and what the reader should do next.
3. `##` Core sections (5-7 H2s):
   - Intent-matched guidance, examples, and concrete numbers.
   - At least one list/table/callout per section.
4. `## What To Do Next`:
   - 3-5 numbered action steps.
5. `## Frequently Asked Questions`:
   - 3-5 questions as `### Question?` with concise answers.

## SEO Requirements
- Word count target: 1200-1800 (adjust by SERP depth).
- Primary keyword in:
  - H1
  - first 100 words
  - at least one H2
  - meta description
- Internal links: 3-5 contextual links.
- External links: 2-3 authoritative sources.
- Meta description: 140-160 chars, no labels in body.

## CTA Requirements
- Exactly 3 CTAs:
  - Early CTA (within first 300 words)
  - Context CTA (mid-article, after actionable details)
  - Final CTA (end of article or FAQ)
- CTA copy must be helpful, not pushy.
- Use `https://claimcoach.app` for main conversion CTA links.

## Interactive Tools (Calculator/Quiz)
- If article includes money math, valuation, thresholds, or "is this fair" intent:
  - embed one mini tool placeholder: `<!-- TOOL:tool_id:mini -->`
  - include one plain link to tools hub: `https://claimcoach.app/tools`
- Pick the most relevant tool for intent:
  - `sales_tax_calculator`
  - `settlement_checklist`
  - `fairness_quiz`
  - `car_worth_estimator`
- Do not force tools into purely narrative content.

## Quality Guardrails
- No placeholder text (for example: "Infographic explaining..." or TODO markers).
- No metadata markers in body (`META_DESCRIPTION:`, `META_TITLE:`, `SLUG:`).
- Keep paragraphs short (2-4 lines), active voice, plain language.
- Never claim ClaimCoach negotiates claims or guarantees payout amounts.
