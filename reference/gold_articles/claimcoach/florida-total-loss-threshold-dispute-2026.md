## Florida Total Loss Threshold: What It Means in Practice

Florida commonly applies an 80% economic threshold framework in total-loss handling contexts. In practice, insurers compare estimated repair cost against pre-loss vehicle value and route the file accordingly.

Sources for consumer verification and state context:

- [Florida Department of Financial Services](https://www.myfloridacfo.com/)

Why this matters to your wallet: if pre-loss value is understated, every downstream decision is biased lower.

## If the Carrier Still Refuses to Move
