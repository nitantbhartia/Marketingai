## What the Florida Total Loss Threshold Actually Controls

In Florida, total-loss handling is tied to statutory and title/salvage requirements. See official references:
- [Florida Statutes](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/)
- [FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/)

For your payout, the most important practical question is not “is it totaled?” It is: **was ACV calculated fairly?**

Your offer depends on:
- Comparable vehicle quality
- Mileage/condition adjustments
- Included tax/fee components
- Documentation of your vehicle’s actual condition

## Where Florida Offers Usually Go Wrong

1. **Trim mismatch**: base trim used against a higher trim vehicle
2. **Mileage drag**: comps with much higher miles than your car
3. **Condition downgrade**: no credit for maintenance and clean condition
4. **Geography drift**: comps from weaker markets far from your buying radius
5. **Missing line items**: taxes and mandatory replacement costs omitted

> **Adjuster Insider:** If you do not challenge comp quality, the first number often becomes the final number.

## The 48-Hour Dispute Workflow

1. Request the valuation report and comp list in writing.
2. Build a two-column comp table: insurer comp vs corrected comp.
3. Attach 3-5 like-for-like local comps.
4. Add receipts/photos that support condition and upgrades.
5. Send a written counteroffer with an exact revised value.

## Example Counteroffer Math

## If the Adjuster Pushes Back

Keep it professional and narrow:
- “Please respond to each disputed comparable line by line.”
- “Please confirm why comp #2 is like-for-like despite trim/mileage differences.”
- “Please provide revised worksheet including tax/fee treatment.”

Written precision beats emotional escalation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I dispute a Florida total loss offer after receiving the first number?
Yes. You can dispute ACV inputs, comp selection, and omitted reimbursement items with documented evidence.

### How many comparables should I provide?
Typically 3-5 strong local comparables are enough to force a meaningful reassessment.

### What usually moves the payout the most?
Correcting trim/mileage comp errors and condition adjustments, then adding missing tax/fee components.
